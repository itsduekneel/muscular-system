Three types of muscle

  1. 01

    Skeletal

    Voluntary and attached to bones. You control these when you walk, jump, or lift.

  2. 02

    Smooth

    Involuntary. Found in the walls of the stomach, intestines, and blood vessels; it moves on its own.

  3. 03

    Cardiac

    Involuntary and found only in the heart. It keeps beating without getting tired.
