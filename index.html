Three Types of Muscle

Skeletal

Voluntary muscles attached to bones. You control them when walking, jumping, or lifting.

Smooth

Involuntary muscles found in the walls of the stomach, intestines, and blood vessels. They work automatically.

Cardiac

Involuntary muscle found only in the heart. It continuously contracts to keep the heart beating.
